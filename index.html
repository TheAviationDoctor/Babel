The seam

        

          

            
            
          

          
The Library has no edge. Walk east past the last hexagon of a floor and you are back at the first; climb past the last floor and you are on the first again. The seam is where these numbers begin again. Nothing marks it, and positions are counted east and up from it, as longitude is counted from Greenwich. The map shows the whole Library, east to the right and up to the top: its border is the seam, and each side joins the side opposite. The dot is this hexagon.

Where it stands

          
The catalogue runs the shelving backwards: it composes the book that begins with your text and finds the shelf it stands on. Every possible book is somewhere in the Library, exactly once.

The catalogue can find any of your own books in the Library: the plain-text files (.txt) in the books folder beside index.html. A page opened straight from your computer cannot look into a folder by itself, so show it the folder once. Your browser may ask whether to “upload” the files: they are only read by this page, and nothing leaves your computer.

The Library of Babel

after Jorge Luis Borges

Every book that could be written in 512 pages stands somewhere on these shelves, exactly once. Walk the galleries, climb the stairs, take down a volume and read it.

The seed decides which book stands on which shelf. The same seed always gives the same Library.
